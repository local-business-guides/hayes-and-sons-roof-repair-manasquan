Questions to ask and details to share

A useful first conversation can help you understand what comes next. Consider asking:

  • What will you look at during the assessment?
  • How will you explain the concern and the proposed repair?
  • What should I do if I notice a change before the visit?
  • Who can I speak with if I have a question afterward?

Share where you noticed the concern and when it first came to your attention. If you've already made a note of changes or taken pictures from a safe spot, those details can help explain what you've seen. You don't need to decide what caused the issue or suggest a repair yourself.

One mistake to avoid is approving work based only on a quick guess. Another is assuming a contractor's online description answers questions about your particular roof. Ask for an explanation tied to the assessment, and make sure you understand the proposed scope before making a decision. If a roof concern changes or you aren't sure what you're seeing, call a professional rather than trying to inspect the roof on your own.
