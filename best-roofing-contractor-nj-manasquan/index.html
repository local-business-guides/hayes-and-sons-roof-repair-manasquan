What to prepare before a roof repair visit

A roof repair starts with a review of the area and a discussion of what you've noticed. The assessment helps identify what may need attention and what work is being proposed. The details depend on the roof and the problem, so don't assume that a single symptom points to one particular repair.

Before you speak with a contractor, make a short checklist:

  • Note when you first noticed the concern and whether it has changed.
  • Write down where you see signs of a problem inside or outside.
  • Take photos from a safe place if they help show what you've observed.
  • List any questions about the assessment, proposed work, and communication.

Avoid climbing onto the roof to investigate. A ground-level view and a clear description are safer starting points. Don't ignore a concern while waiting for a convenient time to ask about it. If you see a new issue or the condition changes, contact a professional to discuss an assessment.
